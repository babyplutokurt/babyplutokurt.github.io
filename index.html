Research

Compression-native genomic computing. Sequencing and pangenome data grow faster than the hardware to analyze them. I build GPU compressors for this data (CuVert-Q, GFAz, GPUFastq, SeqBench), run analytics directly on compressed pangenome graphs, and, most recently, share exact computation across thousands of personal genomes when running deep-learning models, which makes inference up to 584× faster than standard batched execution with identical outputs.

Quantization for efficient LLM training and inference. I have worked on reduced-precision LLM systems across hardware: int8 inference kernels on AMD CPUs at Oracle, LLM serving on TPUs and GPU quantization at Google, and error-bounded compression of KV caches and embeddings in my Ph.D. work. Next, I am interested in quantization-aware RL post-training, FP8/FP4 fine-tuning, and error-bounded KV-cache quantization.
